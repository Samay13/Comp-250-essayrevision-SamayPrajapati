\## Abstract

This paper dives into the how Artificial Intelligence is displayed across social media platforms, looking into its role with content personalization, business automation, sports analytics, and the emerging threat of deepfakes and misinformation.



\## Introduction

Almost everybody scrolls through social media, but few people realize that every post, video, and ad they see is carefully chosen by AI.

In the past couple of years, Artificial Intelligence has become a huge part of how social media platforms work, which influences what people see, how people communicate, and can show bias on what opinions people might have.

Through algorithms, AI can recommend videos, posts, and advertisements by getting information from user data, which shapes people's experiences online without them even realizing.

AI can be used to help society by making jobs easier, but there are also many ways to exploit it in a bad way.

People use AI to help replicate human work, help generate ideas for people, and help recognize patterns within sports events to create highlights and to track stats.

These examples show how AI can be a powerful tool, save time, and provide new possibilities for individuals and businesses.

However, as technology advances and grows, the potential for it to be misused increases.

Although all these things would be helpful to people, AI can also be used to create deepfakes and other fake news that can be send to people who view this with high influence.

When false information spreads online, it can harm reputations of people, influence people onto sides, and make people doubt reliable sources.

The issue isn't exactly with how advanced AI becomes, but how it is used.

So, although the use of AI in social media can greatly help people in everyday life, it also raises significant ethical concerns, such as the spread of misinformation and violation of user privacy that shows the urgent need for responsible AI design and regulation.

Artificial Intelligence has also created social habits that didn't exist in previous years.

People now rely on systems with AI to decide what to watch, read, and even what to believe.

AI targets content to audiences with specific views and feeds them that content.

This means that our attention is being directed by behind-the-scenes AI that learn from our decisions in what we click on online.

Many people don't realize how much of what they are seeing is being fed to them through AI algorithms.

Over time, these algorithms feed people so much of the same content that they can only view one side and not have any information on opposing opinions.

This can increase social division with how people only view one side of an argument and rely on that information.

The conversation about AI in social media is not only about technology itself, but also about human psychology.

Every click or scroll trains these systems to learn more about people, sometimes even better than they know themselves.

As society continues to grow more dependent on AI, being able to understand and regulate their influential power is essential for maintaining freedom of thought.



\## Business and Advertising Automation

AI is used by businesses and industries to improve themselves and increase their potential.

AI has become an essential part of nearly every major industry.

There are algorithms to make predictions, recommend actions, and guide users with decision making at a speed and accuracy that no team made up of humans would be able to match.

Businesses not only use AI as a convenience, but as an advantage.

Now, those who do not implement AI to their business risk falling behind.

For example, companies use machine-learning algorithms to analyze substantial amounts of marketing data in minutes.

This would typically take people days or weeks to finish.

This allows them to adjust advertisements in real time and be able to reach specific audiences more efficiently using that data.

Along with that, AI systems can do research to find which colors, phrases, or images attract viewers, which can help the businesses create better products.

This analysis also affects consumers in ways.

When ads are designed based on what people like, they might buy the product when they typically wouldn't.

This personalization can seem like it is more helpful, but it also raises ethical questions about it being manipulation.

Some companies use AI to help organize their pages and create charts and graphs using data.

There is also a popular Chinese artist whose work has been blended with some AI generated techniques.

The AI helps create ideas and influence from other previous successful works of art, which the artist can then use in his work.

The mix of human creativity with machine analysis shows how AI can be a partner for people rather than a replacement for artists.

It allows for creativity and inspiration from people and the efficiency from AI to produce these works with ease.

The AI is being used in a helpful way by providing ways to improve some work for the artist and the companies.

AI can expand on what humans are capable of creating.

They use human creativity to improve their work.

They work together rather than against each other.

Most companies are now exploring how AI can support their work rather than automation alone.

More development continues to create innovative designs for digital advertising.

AI can also find and use trends and patterns to be able to provide charts and graphs for the companies.

This helps the companies analyze and track data where they get to conclusions quicker.

In addition to this, many companies are turning to AI to take over their customer service.

Chatbots powered by AI are now able to hold conversations with customers and answer their questions.

This saves time for both the businesses and the customers.

Businesses are able to spend their time on greater matters and customers don't have to wait in a queue for someone to answer their call.

Even with these good things, this can grow a dependence on artificial systems, which can sometimes fail to apply some emotional and ethical situations with some of the problems people have.

These systems can help improve productivity for companies but also shows how much AI is changing the way human decision-making works.

While AI is beneficial for these companies, it also brings new risks with it, especially when human judgement is replaced entirely by AI systems.

Overall, this shows that whenever AI is used responsibly, it can help people get away from repetitive work and lets them focus on more creativity and innovation in their work.



\## Sports Analytics and Media

In addition to being used by businesses, AI is changing the world of sports by speeding up analysis of games and content creation.

Modern teams depend on AI algorithms to track how players play, ball movement, and even the crowd, which gives teams a deeper look into each game.

AI has been used in sports where teams can create highlights and get stats from games using it.

AI can also analyze thousands of plays within seconds, which allows teams to be able to look deeper into the games.

Teams can use AI to scan through game footage to find some game highlights, but this also raises the question that if teams can use AI to find weaknesses and other flaws in the opposing teams.

While this possibility can enhance how a team strategizes, it also raises concerns on fairness.

For example, if that equipment is expensive and not accessible for all teams, it can give an unfair edge to teams.

One article says, "We propose an AI-based multimedia production framework for the automatic publishing of soccer and ice hockey highlights on social media, and disseminate our experiences with developing dedicated pipelines for event detection and classification, player detection and tracking, highlight clipping, cropping, thumbnail generation, game summarization, caption generation, and social media sharing" (Midolgu, 2024, pg. 1).

This advancement in technology can help many sports teams as they can review game footage and search for specific details they need to look for.

Teams can find strengths and weaknesses through AI's data from the game footage.

This AI can also help the media aspects of the teams as they will be able to find easy access to game clips and highlights.

They can use these clips to make it easier by making highlight reels and other posts for social media.

This can allow for more personalization with fans by people able to find and generate highlight reels based on favorite teams and players.

It feeds viewers more content in which they are most likely interested.

With this, these systems can reduce the need for editing jobs for people, which creates a concern about employment and the balance of innovation and job security within sports media.

AI has also expanded into sports science, where it is used to analyze players during game days to help prevent injuries and improve performance.

By collecting data from the games, AI can detect patterns in fatigue, strain, and reaction time with specific players.

Using that information, coaches can design safer training schedules with it.

There are also wearable technology where AI can provide real-time feedback to players, which can allow for adjustments to happen in the moment.

These small additions for teams can be the difference in them winning or losing.

With that, there also comes a concern about whether players are comfortable knowing that every single move they do is being tracked and stored.

It gives them less freedom and can make them feel like they are always being watched.

While AI has changed the way the production of sports happens, it also brings up the challenges of fairness, privacy, and equal opportunity.



\## Deepfakes, Misinformation, and Authenticity

Even though AI offers creative disadvantages, it also presents one of the most serious threats to authenticity online.

AI is known to be used for deepfakes and other fake news.

This can lead to a lot of misinformation going around.

Deepfake videos and AI-generated articles can spread a lot of false narratives that can damage reputations or also even influence political outcomes.

This technology is becoming increasingly convincing, even to the point where experts cannot recognize it.

An article says, "As generative AI is deployed to create fake news or deepfakes, these malicious posts become more convincing" (Uesugi, 2024, pg. 1).

This quote shows that AI begins to improve its work to make it more difficult to recognize.

Realism by AI makes it even more difficult for fact-checkers and social media platforms to keep up with it.

Although AI is used for creating fake things, people have now found a way to use AI to detect when material is generated from AI.

People are using AI against AI.

This race shows a greater ethical problem that if people are able to maintain control over tools that are continuously getting better at outsmarting AI detection.

This brings up the question that if AI cannot recognize work from other AI, then would that mean people have no chance of being able to tell what material is made by people or if it's AI generated?

That could become a great problem if AI continues to grow at this rate.

People would not be able to tell what content is real and what is fake.

If this continues at this rate, society can face a future where the truth can be difficult to find, and it would be impossible to trust any source.

This issue has already started to affect elections, news, and campaigns.

When misinformation created by AI spreads quicker than it can be verified, it becomes impossible for the average person to be able to recognize if it is real or not.

During global events, false content created by AI can go viral within hours, which can shape opinions and can also result in conflict.

Along with that, AI also has the ability to mimic human voices.

It allows for scammers to impersonate influential figures or loved ones.

With how big it is becoming, many people can't recognize the difference in some of the real or fake videos.

These situations show why regulation in AI with how it's being used is crucial.

Governments, educators, and social media companies are all beginning to discuss how to combat this issue, but the progress for that is considered slow compared to the speed at which AI is developing.

Studies are occurring to monitor the progress on how AI can mimic human writing styles, art patterns, and emotion.

People are researching if there are patterns with human-generated content and if there are any similarities or differences in patterns with AI-generated content.

By studying these similarities, the researchers are hoping to understand what makes human work unique and whether AI has the ability to truly be able to replicate it.

This study is searching to see that if there are similarities with human-generated content and AI-generated content, it will make it even more difficult to be able to tell when content is made by people or AI.

This type of research also raises questions about originality and uniqueness because if a machine can produce work that is difficult to recognize whether it's human-made or made by AI, would it lower the value of human creativity?

AI continues to try its hardest to replicate human work.

If it continues to improve at the rate, it has been, it might eventually become unrecognizable for work to be made by people or AI.

This shows that society will eventually need new standards and tools to keep the integrity of people's work and be able to trust the sources that are used.

The debate about AI and creativity also touches upon emotional intelligence and meaning.

Human creativity often comes from personal experiences, emotions, and context.

All these things are arguably what makes a human a human.

These things are not qualities that machines can truly feel and replicate.

Even though AI can generate content that looks or sounds real, it typically is missing the emotional depth behind it.

For example, AI can be used to draft a poem that uses the correct rhythm and structure that a poem should have, but it doesn't have the experiences that have inspired poems from real people.

It lacks the realism aspect of it.

It might fit the rubric for what makes a poem a poem, but it might not have the feeling and meaning that humans can incorporate into it.

This also creates a question of whether art without emotion and feelings can still be considered authentic.

AI can also be able to crank out these works with ease and flood the internet.

This can make it more difficult for human artists and writers to gain recognition with their work.

All people would see is AI-generated work all over, while actual real human work gets drained out with how much more AI work there would be.

The market would become greatly filled with cheap, AI-produced content.

This can force society to redefine what it means to value creativity from human work.

As AI becomes more autonomous, the creativity between humans and machines is becoming to be more similar.

Many experts predict that in the future, AI could be able to create and run entire projects, such as news stories or music albums, without any input from humans and they do it on their own.

If AI continues to grow at this rate, it eventually raises the question that if it will learn how to run and create material on its own.

People need to get better at recognizing what content is made from AI or not before it gets to the point of being unrecognizable.

People could do things like developing AI-detection literacy, teaching people to question their sources and analyze them correctly, and it could become as important as teaching a class like math or science.

There are currently research experiments to monitor and track if people can recognize the difference between real and AI-generated content.

With these research experiments, people can monitor how realistic AI can become, but it is up to them to know when to draw the line on when AI can get too powerful.

All these things call for regulation and collaboration.

The automation of AI can greatly affect jobs, education, and even personal identity.

As machines take on roles that are more independent, workers might have to adapt to it by developing skills that complement AI rather than competing against it.

That would allow for them to still have a job where they work alongside with AI instead of getting replaced by it.

Schools might also need to begin teaching classes from AI along with traditional subjects where students can be prepared to work with AI.

It will help them in the future to build their skills around working with AI instead of how it has been today where AI is developing those skills.

If students continue to build their skills as they have been throughout the years, their jobs might be taken over by AI as AI will be able to learn those skills and do them better.

In the workplace though, people must be able to look over AI as they do their work.

Without human oversight, the decisions that the AI can be incorrect sometimes or have errors.

People can never know when AI can become truly a hundred percent correct.

For example, if AI makes a harmful choice such as publishing false information or denying someone access to a service, the question is brought up for who is responsible for that mistake.

The people looking over the AI, or the AI itself?

These issues are crucial for why AI maybe should not become fully independent.

People should ensure that autonomy with AI does not stay unregulated but instead is used to support people in their work.

People and Artificial Intelligence should collaborate with each other rather than working against each other.



\## Conclusion

The discussion around AI in social media is not only about its capabilities, but about how people choose to use them.

Although the use of AI in social media can greatly help people in everyday life, it also raises significant ethical concerns, such as the spread of misinformation and violation of user privacy that shows the urgent need for responsible AI design and regulation.

AI can help replicate human work, generate ideas for people, and recognize patterns in sporting events.

Even with these good things, they still produce many deepfakes and other fake news, are becoming more similar to material made by people, and it can eventually be unrecognizable by people for if it is real or not.

This uncertainty shows the importance of having transparency and accountability with future technological development.

This question of AI becoming too strong is becoming more relevant as time goes on and people need to monitor the rate at how big AI can get.

Although AI can be used in many ways to be helpful to people, it is greatly needed for people to make sure it is not used in bad ways.

For AI to be used as a till for progress in society instead of manipulation, there needs to be more ethical education of this topic, stricter regulations and rules, and awareness would need to be spread to people.

AI represents both the greatest innovation and also one of the biggest challenges of our time.

The influence it has had on social media, businesses, sports, and art shows how much it has become a part of our everyday life.

The decisions that are made today with regulation, education, and transparency will shape how AI would be used in the future.

If AI is used responsibly, it can increase human potential, strengthen communities, and help boost creativity with people.

But if used incorrectly, it can easily break trust with people, fairness, and the truth itself.

Society must approach AI with caution.

We need to recognize that even though AI can help human progress greatly, it can also create more problems in the future.

The future of AI depends not only on what machines can do, but really on what people choose to let them do.
